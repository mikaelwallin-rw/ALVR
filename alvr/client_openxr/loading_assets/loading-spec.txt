ScreenVR loading screen: spec for the headset-rendered transition (v1, 2026-11-10)
Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/G7OFvjP2mAAF6qokxNrKYR  (section D, components on page "Building blocks")

PURPOSE
Replace the magenta (chroma-key) view shown while V3 starts. Rendered by the headset (ALVR client),
language-free (no text, nothing to localise), never black, never magenta.

COLOURS (Tenstar Graphic Profile 2025)
Background        Tenstar Ocean     #40505E  (RGB 64, 80, 94)   opaque, full view
Track             Tenstar Aluminium #E9E9E9  at 18 % opacity   (alpha 46/255)
Arc (progress)    Tenstar Green     #93CB4D  (RGB 147, 203, 77)
Going back        Tenstar Orange    #F3AD2C  (RGB 243, 173, 44)  ring; icon in Aluminium #E9E9E9

PLACEMENT
- World-locked, 2.0 m in front of the seated head position. Yaw = head yaw at the moment the loading
  screen starts (no pitch/roll). Centre 5 degrees below eye level. Not head-locked.
- Quad 0.48 m x 0.48 m (13.7 deg) carrying the 1024x1024 texture.
- Ring outer diameter 0.41 m = 11.7 deg (~12 deg); stroke 0.034 m (~1 deg). Well inside the central 30 deg.
- Logo (decided): the original artwork D:\wkspaces\ScreenVR\Logos\Tenstar_Logo_Alu-2023 (4).png
  ("TENSTAR" secondary logo, Aluminium version for dark backgrounds; 3048 x 844 px, tightly cropped,
  aspect 3.61). Never redraw, recolour or crop it. The "Tenstar simulation" version is not used here
  because "simulation" would be too small at this size (graphic profile: secondary logo for small formats).
  World: a separate quad in the same world-locked plane as the ring, 0.28 m x 0.078 m (8.0 deg wide),
  centre 0.31 m below the ring centre (about 13.6 deg below eye level). Gap from the ring's outer edge to
  the logo's top edge: 0.065 m. Clear space: at least 1/4 of the logo height (0.02 m) free on every side
  (the profile gives no figure; this is the rule used on the board). Opacity follows the ring
  (fades in and out with it). Restrained on purpose: ring + logo only, nothing else.
  Eye-buffer reference (1024, 100 deg): logo ~60 x 17 px, centre at (512, 616); ring centre at (512, 550).

LAYERS (top to bottom)
1. Tracked hands
2. Touchscreen virtual copy at its real pose, live video, fully usable (never hidden, never moved)
3. Indicator quad and logo quad
4. Ocean background (opaque, full view). During cross-fades, passthrough or the simulator frame blends with it.

TEXTURE GEOMETRY (1024 x 1024, transparent)
- Centre (512, 512). Ring centreline radius 402 px, stroke 72 px -> outer diameter 876 px, inner 732 px.
- Arc: 100 deg, centred at 12 o'clock in the static asset, round caps.
- 512 x 512 versions: everything halved (r 201 px, stroke 36 px).
- Hourglass (taking longer): Aluminium, stroke 28 px, inside the ring, 200 x 260 px.
- Return arrow (going back): Aluminium, stroke 36 px, inside an all-Orange ring.

ANIMATION (normal)
- Rotation: clockwise, constant 180 deg/s (one turn per 2 s).
- Arc length: L(t) = 105 deg - 45 deg * cos(pi * t), t in seconds -> 60..150 deg, ease-in-out sine, period 2 s.
- Arc centre angle: theta(t) = -90 deg + 180 deg/s * t (screen coordinates, 0 deg = 3 o'clock, clockwise).
- 12 key frames per cycle (every 1/6 s): k = 0..11, t = k/6, rotation = 30k deg,
  arc = 60, 66, 83, 105, 128, 144, 150, 144, 128, 105, 83, 66 deg.
- Minimal implementation: rotate loading-indicator-arc over loading-indicator-track at 180 deg/s
  (fixed 100 deg arc, no length animation).

APPEAR / DISAPPEAR
- Indicator fade-in 0.3 s, scale 0.92 -> 1.0, ease-out, starting when passthrough is 50 % faded.
- Indicator fade-out 0.3 s while the simulator's first frame cross-fades in.
- Minimum visible time 0.6 s (no flashes).

TIMELINE: ENTERING VR
Context (decided): V3 does its long loading BEFORE it connects to the VR runtime. During that time the
trainee stays in the front end in passthrough with all screens. The loading screen only covers the last
seconds, from the VR connection to V3's first frame. Times below are relative to the VR connection.
Start        Tap Start on the real touchscreen (passthrough, all screens); V3 loads in the background.
0-0.3 s      (from VR connect) Main screen copies fade out. Touchscreen copy stays.
0.3-0.8 s    Passthrough fades into the Ocean background (0.5 s, ease-in-out). Indicator fades in.
0.8 s ->     Loading screen until V3's first frame (passthrough off; no magenta at any time).
+0-0.5 s     First simulator frame cross-fades in (0.5 s); indicator fades out.

TIMELINE: LEAVING VR
0-0.5 s      Simulator cross-fades into the loading screen; indicator fades in.
-> front end Loading screen while V3 closes and the front end returns.
+0-0.5 s     Passthrough fades in (0.5 s); indicator fades out.
+0.5-0.8 s   Main screen copies fade in (0.3 s) at their stored positions. No calibration.

VARIANTS (all language-free)
Time-outs are fixed (decided), counted from the VR connection.
- Taking longer (from 15 s): hourglass fades into the ring centre (0.5 s) and turns over every 4 s
  (0.6 s ease-in-out). Ring keeps turning.
- Going back (V3 exits before its first frame, or no first frame after 60 s): arc stops,
  ring cross-fades to Orange (0.3 s), return arrow appears; hold 1.5 s; passthrough fades in (0.5 s)
  with all screens. Only afterwards may a localised headset panel say "The exercise could not start."

WHAT THE INSTRUCTOR SEES (decided: nothing extra needed)
- A window on the normal monitors mirrors the trainee's VR view at all times, including the loading
  screen, the hourglass and the going-back state.
- Physical touchscreen: front end, then the in-cab touch UI when V3 starts it; on failure, the front end again.
